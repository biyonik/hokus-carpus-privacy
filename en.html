Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

01Data we collect 02Ads 03Purchases 04Parental controls 05Deleting data 06Changes and contact

Hokus Çarpus is an educational game for children, developed by Ahmet Altun. Contact: ahmet.altun60@gmail.com.

01Data we collect

The app does not collect personal information. It never asks for or stores your child’s name, photo, voice, location, contacts or advertising ID. Progress and settings are stored only on this device, encrypted, and never sent to a server. We use no analytics or crash reporting tools.

02Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent; content is limited to General audiences, and ads are not personalized. The advertising ID permission is removed. AdMob may process technical data such as IP address and device information for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and diagnostics.

03Purchases

The Parent Pack is bought through Google Play. Google handles payment details; we never see them. The ad system is not started for pack owners.

04Parental controls

Purchases, language changes, links and resetting progress are behind a parental gate. Daily play time can be limited in the grown-ups’ corner.

05Deleting data

Grown-ups’ corner → Reset progress, or uninstalling the app, deletes all local data.

06Changes and contact

Policy changes will be published on this page. Questions: ahmet.altun60@gmail.com.
